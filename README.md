# Ocular Electrooculography (EOG) & Arden Ratio Skill

A production-grade, zero-mock personal agent skill engineered for ocular electrophysiological assessment, retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) standing potential dynamics, and macular dystrophy evaluation.

## Features
- **Deterministic Arden Ratio Calculation**: Computes exact light peak / dark trough ratios ($LP / DT$) and percentage indexes.
- **ISCEV Standard Compliance**: Follows International Society for Clinical Electrophysiology of Vision protocols.
- **Pure Standard Library**: Zero external dependencies; fully portable for edge and clinical AI deployment.

## Clinical References
- Constable, P. A., et al. (2017). *ISCEV Standard for clinical electro-oculography (2017 update)*. Documenta Ophthalmologica, 134(1), 1-9.
- Arden, G. B., & Kelsey, J. H. (1962). *Changes produced by light in the standing potential of the eye*. The Journal of Physiology, 161(2), 189-204.
